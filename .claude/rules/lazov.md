# Lažov (id `fibbage`)

**Lažov** (id is `fibbage`) — write a convincing fake answer to a trivia question, then pick the real one out of the pile. Content is **file-backed** like the other trivia games: packs live in `fibbage-packs/<id>.json` (`{ name?, description?, questions:[{text, answer, category?, accept?}] }`), are edited in `/admin` → Lažov, and are validated by [fibbage-import.ts](../../packages/shared/src/games/fibbage-import.ts). A manifest **carries the answers**, so it follows the kviz split rather than the Ko sam ja one: `GET /api/fibbage-packs` serves summaries only and the chosen ids ride `host:start-game` as `fibbagePackIds` to be resolved server-side ([fibbage-pack-resolver.ts](../../packages/server/src/game/games/fibbage/fibbage-pack-resolver.ts)). `FIBBAGE_QUESTION_BANK` stays in code purely as the **fallback** for an empty/bad pack folder — packs load asynchronously, so `showing-question` holds while `questionsReady` is false instead of running the writing timer against a question nobody has seen. Three scoring rules carry the whole game and none of them are visible from the screen, which is why [scripts/test-fibbage.mts](../../scripts/test-fibbage.mts) exists: **the truth bonus is paid once** — a player who types the real answer is an *auto-finder*, banks `TRUTH_POINTS` and is excluded from `expectedVoterIds` entirely (letting them vote as well paid it twice); **writing is the price of scoring** — someone who sits out the writing phase still votes but earns nothing for finding the truth, or doing nothing becomes the strongest play; and **identical lies merge into one option** that pays every author. The reveal attributes **every** lie, including the ones nobody picked (`revealOptions`, sorted by the module: 0-vote lies first, then rising vote count, truth last), and then spells out **who fooled whom** in words on all three surfaces ([FoolsBoard.tsx](../../packages/host/src/games/fibbage/components/FoolsBoard.tsx) on the TV, named lines on the hostless strip, and `fooledNames` / `fooledByNames` on the phone's own result card). The avatars beside each option are not enough on their own — across a room you register *that* somebody fell for a lie without ever registering *who*, which is the one thing players repeat out loud afterwards. `voterNames` rides on the reveal option itself precisely so all three render it from one field: the phone has no player roster and the hostless strip would otherwise join against the leaderboard. A round where nothing landed says so rather than rendering an empty section, and `SHOWING_RESULTS_DURATION` went 8 s → 10 s to fit the extra block. Headless end-to-end + anti-leak run: `npx tsx scripts/test-fibbage.mts` (`--players 4..8`, `--rounds n`, `--pack id`; a nonexistent `--pack` exercises the built-in-bank fallback).
