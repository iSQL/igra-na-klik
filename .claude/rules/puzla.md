# Puzla

**Puzla** — collaborative jigsaw from a picture the host uploads at game-select; every phone renders the **whole table** (pan / pinch / finger drag) in TV rooms too, and the TV only mirrors it, so it is hostless-capable. 1–8 players (`validateStart` enforces the 8), 16/36/64/100 nominal pieces (the real grid follows the aspect, e.g. 96), optional rotated pieces (tap rotates), and `vreme` (hardcoded deadline by piece count, ×1.5 with rotation) vs `opusteno` (untimed). **The picture never touches disk**: it travels over `host:puzla-image` — the platform's only socket **ack** — and lives in an in-memory per-room store ([puzla-image-store.ts](../../packages/server/src/game/games/puzla/puzla-image-store.ts)) served at `/puzla-slika/<room>/<uuid>` and deleted in `destroyRoom`. The client re-encodes to a JPEG ≤ `PUZLA_CLIENT_TARGET_BYTES` (340KB) because socket.io over long-polling base64s binary and anything past `maxHttpBufferSize` kills the transport **without an ack**; the server re-checks (≤360KB, JPEG magic, size read from the SOF marker — never the client's claim, aspect 1:3–3:1), answers every refusal through the ack, and refuses uploads outside the lobby (a re-upload would swap the id under a running puzzle). Start carries `puzlaImageId`; a stale id is refused with a clear message, which is what keeps the lobby rematch honest. **Shapes are never on the wire**: `hostData.geo` carries a seed and every client derives the same Draradech-style tab edges via `tracePuzlaPiece` ([puzla-rules.ts](../../packages/shared/src/games/puzla-rules.ts)); the server never needs a shape, because a group's position is the world position of its **grid origin** — pieces sit right relative to each other exactly when their groups share origin and rotation, so snapping ([snap.ts](../../packages/server/src/game/games/puzla/snap.ts), pure) is "origins within 0.35 cell + grid-adjacent". Group 0 is the frame; a drop locks into it with a border piece or by touching a locked one (an interior piece has nothing to square against). Credit is 5 per new neighbour pair + 10 per lock, so it can't be farmed; completion pays everyone 100 (+ up to 200 for time left). **Networking follows Splav's split**: `grab`/`move` return null and positions of held groups ride `game:frame` (~15/s, `tickIntervalMs` 66) — **built at tick time from current positions, never captured when a move arrives**, or a frame sent after a drop's full state drags the piece back — and every frame carries *all* held groups so a reconnecting phone is right within one frame; `drop`, `rotate`, releases and phases return a full state (the cached state is what reconnects replay). A hold is released without a snap when the holder's socket drops (checked in `onTick` — `onPlayerDisconnect` only fires after the 5-min grace), when they are gone, or after 10 s idle. On the phone the dragging device owns its group (`PuzlaTable.localGroup`) until the drop is confirmed by the state or a frame that no longer lists it, resends the drop after 800 ms, and rolls back if a frame shows another holder. `puzlaTable.ts` (model + sprite atlas + renderer) is an **identical copy** in host and controller, as are `utils/puzlaImage.ts` and `components/PuzlaImagePicker.tsx` — change both. Headless run: `npx tsx scripts/test-puzla.mts` (`--players 1..7` + a guest who disconnects mid-hold, `--pieces`, `--rotation`, `--relaxed`, `--hostless`, `--full` adds the 10 s idle-release check); it covers edge symmetry, the snap rules, layout fit for every count and aspect, upload refusals, frame rate vs full states while dragging and the whole solve.
